2. Information We Collect

nibby collects limited, non-sensitive information to personalize your experience and generate meal recommendations. This may include:

  • Mood, cravings, and dietary preferences you enter.
  • Email address (if you choose to save your plans or journal entries).

We do not collect financial information, health records, or precise location data.
